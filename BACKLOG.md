# Backlog / Future Research

Backlog is not the roadmap. Items here are explicitly out of current implementation scope until `docs/02-roadmap/development-plan.md` promotes them into a phase and `CURRENT.md` names them as current work.

## Rule

If an idea enters Backlog, do not implement it in the current phase unless it is required to finish the active Definition of Done.
