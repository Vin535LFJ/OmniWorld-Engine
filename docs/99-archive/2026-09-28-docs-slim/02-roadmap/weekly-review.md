# Weekly Review

Use this every week. Keep the final answer to one `Next Week #1 Priority`.

## Review Questions

1. What did I complete this week?
2. What did I learn?
3. What did I only read about but not actually master?
4. What did I verify through code?
5. What did I verify through benchmark?
6. What was the biggest blocker?
7. Did I drift away from the main route?
8. What is the single most important thing next week?
